## Mobarmej · مبرمج

**Software engineering from Libya.** We build Arabic-first websites, ERP systems (Odoo, ERPNext) and banking & payments integrations for Libyan businesses.

> نبني مواقع وأنظمة ERP وحلول مصرفية للشركات الليبية، بالعربية أولاً.

### What we do

- **Websites & platforms**: Laravel, Arabic/RTL-first, fast on Libyan networks
- **ERP**: Odoo and ERPNext implementation, localization and integration
- **Payments & banking**: ISO 8583 / ISO 20022 integration, wallets, Central Bank of Libya interfaces
